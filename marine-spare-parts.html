A better brief

What to include in a spare-parts RFQ

Attach the original technical documents and review any extracted information before submitting your request.

  1. Identify the equipment

    Manufacturer, model, serial number and the complete part number. Include a drawing position or catalogue page when the reference is ambiguous.

  2. Build your parts list

    Use one row per item, with quantity and unit. Upload an Excel sheet, PDF, Word document or clear photograph with the original references.

  3. Set the delivery requirement

    Name the vessel, delivery country and port, required date and any known port-call window. State whether partial supply is acceptable.

  4. Explain acceptable alternatives

    Tell us if you require the specified part only or would like alternatives reviewed. An alternative must be checked for compatibility before acceptance.

Before you send your request

Can I request several spare parts together?

Yes. Use a parts list for a multi-item RFQ and review the extracted rows before requesting indicative prices or submitting. Check part numbers, quantities and units against your original document.

Do I need an account to send an RFQ?

Instant Quotation accepts requests without a login. A free Vessel Passport account gives you a workspace for your vessels, equipment records, requests and messages.

Is an indicative estimate an official offer?

No. Any displayed estimate is indicative. Compatibility, availability, delivery timing, taxes and freight need confirmation in the official quotation.
