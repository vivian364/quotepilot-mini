A better brief

Before requesting marine HVAC parts or support

Attach the original technical documents and review any extracted information before submitting your request.

  1. Capture the complete nameplate

    Include manufacturer, model, serial number, voltage, phase and frequency where shown. For cooling equipment, include the recorded refrigerant designation.

  2. Describe the installation

    State the vessel type, equipment location and system arrangement. If considering a replacement, include available dimensions and connection details.

  3. Separate symptoms from the requested part

    Provide alarm codes and observed behaviour, alongside any specific part reference. This helps the team understand whether you need sourcing or a technical assessment.

  4. Give a workable delivery or service window

    Add the vessel, delivery country, port and required date. Specify whether equipment can be accessed during the planned call and include a contact for coordination.

Before you send your request

Can yacht and tender owners submit requests?

Yes. Submit a guest RFQ or create a Vessel Passport account. Include the vessel type and the installed HVAC equipment details; a vessel brand alone may not identify the air conditioning system.

Can a photo identify the correct replacement?

A clear photo and nameplate are useful starting points. They do not by themselves confirm compatibility. Check the model, electrical specification, dimensions and connections with the technical team.

Are local-market prices and delivery guaranteed?

No. Displayed estimates, where available, are indicative. The team confirms the proposed supply or service scope, price, availability and delivery arrangements in the official quotation.
